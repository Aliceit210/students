About This Portfolio

Creativity Meets Professional Training

This portfolio represents the work of a graphic design student trained at Up Skills Hub under the mentorship of Alice Uwimana. Through structured, hands-on training, each project showcases the ability to solve visual communication problems with clarity, creativity, and confidence.

The focus is on building brands that speak, designing visuals that convert, and creating content that connects. Every piece here is more than decoration — it is strategy in visual form.
